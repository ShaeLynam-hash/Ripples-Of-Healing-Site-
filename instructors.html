Meet the Instructors

Certified, passionate, and deeply committed to your well-being — every instructor is hand-selected for their expertise and their humanity.

Anna Fernandez

Founder

Anna has been practicing yoga for over 25 years and teaching since 2013. Her journey began with healing chronic pain from scoliosis and grew into a passion for helping others access their own healing power. A mother of two and founder of Ripples of Healing Online Community and Pier To Point Wellness and Spa in San Diego, she teaches gentle, nourishing classes that create space in the body, mind, and energy, easing stress and tension. Anna is dedicated to building a global community rooted in connection, healing, and compassion, where friends and families can practice together online, no matter the distance.

Amy Freeman

Amy brings over 30 years of experience practicing and teaching yoga. She leads heart-centered classes in Hatha, Vinyasa, and restorative yoga, helping students reconnect with their body, mind, and energy. Her teaching blends movement, breath, and mindfulness to cultivate calm, resilience, and balance, honoring both stillness and motion. A devoted mother of two, Amy brings a grounded, empathetic presence that supports the Ripples of Healing community in experiencing the transformative power of yoga for lifelong well-being.

Alisha Ayaad

Alisha's path in yoga and the healing arts is rooted in a deep desire to help others reconnect with themselves. She teaches Yin yoga with a grounded, intuitive approach informed by massage therapy and traditional Eastern healing practices. Her classes invite students to slow down, reset the nervous system, and restore balance in a busy world, creating space to feel centered, peaceful, and connected in body, mind, and energy.

Brent Honnerlaw

Brent is an experienced yoga teacher (E-RYT 500) who blends Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin, Restorative, meditation, and Qi Gong to create classes that support versatility, balance, and mindful presence. He integrates sound, storytelling, and yoga philosophy into his teaching to help students deepen awareness, reconnect with their bodies, and cultivate calm, focus, and resilience. Brent brings a compassionate, grounded presence to every class, inspiring students to carry the benefits of yoga both on and off the mat.

Carli Klingerman

Carli's teaching began as a personal transformation and quickly became a calling. Her breath-led flows move with a steady, energizing rhythm — building heat, resilience, and creative momentum while staying deeply grounded. With an intuitive balance of strength and surrender, she creates space to move with intention, soften when needed, and reconnect to the body's natural intelligence.

Cynthia Tavares

Cintia brings over 20 years of experience guiding students through yoga practices that cultivate strength, balance, and inner awareness. She leads Vinyasa and Hatha-based classes that blend mindful movement, breathwork, and relaxation in an accessible way for all levels. Her teaching supports physical vitality, mental clarity, and renewed energy, helping students feel grounded, centered, and refreshed both on and off the mat.

India Bee

India shares practices that nourish the nervous system and invite deep rest and renewal. With over 20 years of study, she teaches yoga and yoga nidra in a way that is accessible, grounding, and supportive for all levels. Her classes blend breath, mindfulness, and gentle movement to cultivate calm, resilience, and connection. India believes that if you can breathe, you can practice yoga, and she welcomes students into a space of ease, presence, and community.

Jennifer Opaka

Jenny brings over 30 years of experience in movement and fitness, specializing in mat Pilates and functional strength practices that support alignment, mobility, and whole-body wellness. Her classes are intentional, accessible, and designed to help students feel stronger, more stable, and connected in their bodies. During her own healing journey through cancer, Jenny created Roll & Release Balls, a therapeutic method using small balls to ease tension, restore movement, and support recovery. She brings resilience, compassion, and lived experience into every class, empowering students to move with confidence and care.

Nam Chanterrwyn

Nam is an advanced yoga teacher trained in functional movement and anatomy, known for blending strength, precision, and mindful awareness. His classes are rooted in clear guidance and intentional sequencing, helping students build resilience, improve mobility, and deepen their understanding of movement. Whether teaching Vinyasa or restorative practices, Nam supports students in exploring their strength, sharpening focus, and developing lasting tools they can carry beyond the mat. With experience working alongside professional athletes and everyday practitioners alike, he brings a thoughtful, educational approach that empowers students to move with confidence and clarity.

Olivia Hughes

Olivia has taught yoga and Reiki since 2011, offering a grounded, heart-centered approach shaped by years of study and service. She is certified in Vinyasa, Hatha, Yin, and Aerial Yoga, spent seven years co-training aerial teachers certifying 150+ students, and has been a Reiki Master since 2018, certifying more than 100 practitioners. She has co-led international retreats for over a decade and holds a master's degree in Spiritual Psychology, which informs her workshops, mentorship, and signature Alchemy of Healing experience. Olivia is also the co-founder of Yoga in the Park, a thriving community class of 150+ students now in its 13th year. Above all, she is a devoted mother guided by service, compassion, and purpose.
