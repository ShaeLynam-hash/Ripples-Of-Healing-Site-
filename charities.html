Protecting & Healing Children
National Children's Alliance
NCA

Supports a national network of Child Advocacy Centers, providing children who have experienced abuse with medical care, counseling, and legal support in a safe and compassionate environment. NCA ensures that every child who has experienced abuse can access the care and healing they deserve — no matter where they live.

Fostering Mental Health & Emotional Well-Being
The Jed Foundation
JED

Works with schools and communities to promote mental wellness, prevent suicide, and equip young people with the life skills to thrive. JED is dedicated to protecting emotional health and preventing suicide for our nation's teens and young adults — giving them the tools and community they need to build resilience and hope.

Supporting At-Risk Youth & Women

○ Your membership supports both organizations below.

Sister League of San Diego

Provides safe housing and life skills programs for women recovering from trauma, helping them regain independence and confidence. Their programs have positively transformed the lives of women in our community — giving them a safe place to heal, grow, and reclaim their lives.

"I have personally worked with Sister League and witnessed firsthand the profound difference they make. Their compassion and dedication to helping women heal is something I deeply believe in." — Anna Fernandez, Founder
G.R.A.C.E.
Girls Rising Above Childhood Exploitation

Offers mentorship, educational programs, and supportive resources for victims of sex trafficking and at-risk youth, helping them gain the tools and support they need to thrive. G.R.A.C.E. meets young people where they are — with compassion, safety, and a roadmap toward a better future.

"The courage and resilience of the young people G.R.A.C.E. serves is extraordinary. Supporting their work is one of the most meaningful things we can do together as a community." — Anna Fernandez, Founder
